About

A specialist translator, not a generalist for hire.

I am a freelance English ↔ Chinese translator and MTPE post-editor based in Shenyang, Liaoning, working with language service providers and clients across North America and Europe.

Since March 2013 I have translated nearly 9 million words. My degree research was on English–Chinese legal texts, and legal, healthcare, and financial & government work remain the core of my practice — documents where a mistranslated term can change an obligation, a benefit, or an official outcome.

I work in both directions and in both scripts, so a single project — a contract, a patient-facing document, a tax notice — stays consistent from first draft to final review.
